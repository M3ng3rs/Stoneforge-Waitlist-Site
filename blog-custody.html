Trust · Oct 5, 2026

Your broker holds the money.

This is the section that decides whether someone with real capital types their email. Features don’t win that moment. Answers do.

StoneForge never receives deposits. We never route withdrawals. Funds stay at your brokerage. The connection is trade-only keys, with withdrawals blocked at the broker by design.

New accounts start in paper mode. Live capital only after you flip the switch yourself. Autonomy is opt-in and bounded by the rules you set.

If we can’t say these three things plainly, we don’t ship. Custody is not a footnote — it’s the product.
